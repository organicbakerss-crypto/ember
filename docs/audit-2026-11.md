# Audit, November 2026

Every area was driven in the real app (scripted, with screenshots): the bookshelf, all three kinds of story, writing (binder, arcs, formatting, find and replace, history, Rhythm/Echo, diagnostics, focus mode, palette, ⋯ menu, export, status bar, the world panel, linked names), World mode (Where/Who/When/How, board, family tree, entry pages, timeline, inbox), every Settings page, the trash, the AI pane and voice. Nothing crashed and no screen logged an error. What's below is what's wrong, unclear, cluttered or missing.

## 1. Broken or wrong

1. **Settings can't be reached from World mode** (only the bookshelf and the writing view have the gear).
2. **New status bar items appear on the right** instead of joining the left group.
3. **People board dragging isn't smooth.**
4. **History lists an empty "0 words" version** the first time a chapter is opened (the version before anything was typed). It's noise.
5. **Two Settings pages are dead ends**: "Keyboard shortcuts" and "Optional modules" are greyed out with "LATER".
6. **Reading aloud on Linux with the computer's own voice doesn't work** (Electron doesn't reach Linux's speech system); it now stops and explains, and VoiceStudio works.
7. **Rename shows "F3"** in the binder menu; F2 is the usual rename key everywhere else.

## 2. Not intuitive

1. **The "//" button** at the top of the writing view (it puts the world beside the page): a symbol with no meaning. Same job as the globe in the left rail, which opens a _different_ thing (the world side panel). Two world buttons, two behaviours.
2. **Diagnostics has no button**: only the command palette opens it. Rhythm and Echo are on the toolbar; their summary panel isn't.
3. **The ⋯ menu mixes everything**: export, import, Royal Road, five voice items, six AI items, chapter headings, "Finished". 17 items with no grouping.
4. **"How"** as a world section name doesn't say what's in it (magic, religions, laws, technology). Where/Who/When are clear; How needs a hint or a better name.
5. **Two toolbar dropdowns with no labels**: "Theme font" and "Default" (the second is text size). They read as settings, not as what they change.
6. **Placing an event on the timeline** is a dropdown under "Not on the timeline yet"; dragging works for "Before the story" but the screen doesn't say so until you look closely.
7. **Find is "Find in volume" only**: no way to search the whole story or the world from the same box (the palette searches the world, but nothing says so).
8. **Many features live only in the command palette (⌘K)** with nothing on screen pointing to it. New users won't find them.
9. **The series page is bare**: a cover, a world link, "Import from Word", and volume cards. No word counts, no progress, no description.

## 3. Clutter

1. The **⋯ menu** (above).
2. **Settings → Appearance**: 24 themes as a plain list of radio buttons with no preview. A grid of small preview cards would be shorter and more useful.
3. The **"LATER" Settings pages** (above).
4. The **AI pane's footer** ("ChatGPT (your plan) · Nothing in your chapter changes. Saved with this story.") repeats on every view; useful once, noise after.
5. The **toolbar** gives as much room to Rhythm/Echo as to bold/italic; formatting and analysis could be grouped apart.

## 4. Missing (that most writing or worldbuilding apps have)

Ordered by how much it would help, for a novelist posting on Royal Road.

**Writing**

1. **Chapter synopsis and notes.** Chapters already store notes, but nothing shows them. Scrivener, Novelcrafter, Plottr all have a synopsis per chapter. The AI summaries could fill it automatically.
2. **An outline / corkboard view**: every chapter as a card (title, synopsis, POV, word count, status), drag to reorder. The binder is a list of titles only.
3. **Chapter status**: draft / revised / done (and colour labels). Also what the publishing module would build on.
4. **Comments on the text**: highlight a passage and leave a note to self ("fix this later"). Standard in Scrivener, Word, Google Docs, Novelcrafter.
5. **Writing stats over time**: words per day, a streak, and a deadline goal ("finish by 1 March → 1,200 words a day"). Today there's only a session and project goal.
6. **Search and replace across the whole story** (all volumes), not one volume.
7. **Split a chapter at the cursor, merge two chapters, duplicate a chapter.**
8. **Backups to a folder**: a zip of the library now and then (sync is not a backup: a deletion syncs too). Most writing apps do this.
9. **Typewriter scrolling** (the line you type stays mid-screen) and a true full-screen mode.
10. **A shortcuts list** (or turn the dead Settings page into it).

**World**

11. **A search box in World mode**: today it's the palette only.
12. **Tags on entries** (e.g. "noble houses", "dead", "book 2"), and filtering by them.
13. **More than one picture per entry** (a gallery: outfits, maps of a house, reference art). Only a portrait today.
14. **Free notes / lore pages** not tied to a template ("History of the Isles", "Calendar"). The How section partly covers this.
15. **Export the world** (as a PDF or a set of pages): World Anvil and Campfire both do it; useful for sharing a series bible.

**Already planned**: the publishing tracker and author's notes (milestone 14) cover what Royal Road writers usually want next.

## 5. Suggested order

1. **Layout round** (planned): Settings everywhere, the ⋯ menu grouped, status bar order, smoother board, plus from this audit: a Diagnostics button, one clear "world" button, labelled toolbar dropdowns, the "LATER" pages removed (or the shortcuts list), F2 for rename, theme previews.
2. **Chapter synopsis + outline/corkboard + chapter status** (one piece: they share a view; the AI summaries fill the synopses).
3. **Comments on text.**
4. **Writing stats and deadline goals.**
5. **Story-wide search and replace; split/merge/duplicate chapters.**
6. **World search box, tags, picture gallery.**
7. **Backups to a folder.**
8. **Milestone 14** (publishing tracker, author's notes) then the leftovers.
